02 / FOLLOW THE BLOOD

Two circuits.
One continuous journey.

01 / BODY → RIGHT HEART

Return & receive

Oxygen-poor blood returns through the venae cavae to the right atrium, then crosses the tricuspid valve into the right ventricle.

02 / RIGHT HEART → LUNGS

Pump to the lungs

The right ventricle sends blood through the pulmonary valve and pulmonary arteries to the lungs, where it gains oxygen.

03 / LUNGS → LEFT HEART

Oxygen-rich return

Pulmonary veins bring blood to the left atrium. It passes through the mitral valve into the left ventricle.

04 / LEFT HEART → BODY

Launch the pulse

The left ventricle ejects blood through the aortic valve into the aorta. ARTERIO studies the optical pulse recorded at a fingertip.

BodyParts3D 4.0 mesh subset prepared by OrganUI. The lower atlas uses source-mapped geometry with a shared display proportion adjustment; the opening point cloud preserves the original procedural art. ARTERIO supplies the rendering and interaction. The atlas is an educational illustration; its display proportions are adapted and are not anatomical measurements. Simplified adult reference with source-mapped valve assemblies, pulmonary vessels , coronary veins and arch branches added from the official BodyParts3D archive. Vessel ends are clipped to the cardiac region. Small distal branches, tissue layers and microscopic anatomy are not complete; no clinical anatomy review is claimed.

The ventricular wall is one source mesh, including its internal muscle. Cavity surfaces depict chamber spaces, not tissue. Cavities and internal valve assemblies are isolated when selected so the exterior wall does not hide them. Study the septum and the full circulation pathway in section 02’s labeled cross section.
